# Credits — Curiosity's Doors

Accurate, per-piece attribution for every asset and role in the game.
Kept current as assets land. Two destinations:

1. **The in-game credits roll** — plays after "The End".
2. **College portfolio submissions** — NYU requires credits per piece and will
   not review uncredited work. See *Submission formats* at the bottom: the
   colleges want this information in three different shapes, none of which is
   the roll.

## Submission formats

The colleges do **not** all want credits the same way. The in-game roll satisfies
none of them on its own — it's for players.

| Where | Shape | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **NYU Tisch** | Full per-piece credits, attached to **every** piece in the portfolio | Hard gate: *"Any pieces that do not include credits will not be reviewed."* Solo work may only be called solo if it truly was. They state outright that too much information beats too little. |
| **SCAD** | A brief prose description of your role(s) in the production | Must accompany the submission. Prose, not a table. |
| **Northeastern** | Your role, specified on each sample | One line per piece, across 1–5 pieces. |
| **USC** | Position + development environment, inside their Creative Portfolio List format | e.g. *"2026, Curiosity's Doors, browser game (Godot 4.6 / GDScript). Position: designer, creative director…"* |
| **UCLA** | Nothing stated | No credits requirement in the supplement. |
| **In-game roll** | Full credits, styled | Plays after "The End". Not a substitute for any of the above. |
